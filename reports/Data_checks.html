Daoukro: why free-product provision is undefined

Undefined does not mean 0% free. It means there are no valid Yes/No product responses from which to calculate a free-product proportion. Daoukro is geographically matched; the problem is the information available for particular supply measures.

Eligibility clarification

The current Côte d’Ivoire filter uses s2_5_sp = 1, reporting FP services generally. This does not establish on-site FP provision. The separate s5_1 question asks about on-site services. Earlier descriptions of the selected filter as confirming on-site provision were too broad.

1. What the included facilities reported

Daoukro has 21 source facility records. Nineteen answer Yes to s2_5_sp and are included in the current supply summaries; two answer No and are excluded.

QuestionResponses among the 19 included facilities
s2_5_sp: Does the health facility provide FP services?19 Yes
s5_1: Does the facility offer FP services on-site?19 No
s5_6: Does the facility offer local FP services, such as outreach or awareness activities?14 Yes; 5 No

The outreach responses help explain how some facilities could report FP services generally while reporting no on-site provision. These answers do not fully establish every facility’s circumstances.

2. What the free-product responses show

Product-specific Q5.3 items ask whether the item is provided free of charge. The recorded categories are 1 = Yes, 2 = No, and −9 = Not applicable. A blank is also unresolved. These are different response categories.

The other product-specific free-provision questions likewise contain no Yes or No responses from these 19 facilities. This pattern is consistent with the questions being inapplicable after no on-site FP provision was reported. Confirming the exact skip instruction, and whether it was applied correctly, requires the original routing instructions or data-team clarification.

3. How the UoM facility proportion is calculated

The original code counts recognized responses on its selected product items:

  • t_free_prod: number recorded Yes.
  • t_not_free_prod: number recorded No, or Never where applicable.
p_free_prod = Yes count ÷ (Yes count + No/Never count)
For each Daoukro facility: 0 ÷ (0 + 0) = 0 ÷ 0 → undefined.

The zero numerator means no affirmative free-product answers were counted; without valid answers, it does not establish that no products are free. The zero denominator means there are no product responses classifiable as free or nonfree. It does not establish that the facility physically has zero products.

Not applicable responses are not counted as No. The cleaned proportion is therefore undefined and represented as missing. The same absence of valid values is present in the originally uploaded cleaned facility file and geographic export.

4. A genuine zero is different

The following examples are hypothetical and illustrate the distinction:

Recorded product answersCalculationResult and interpretation
0 Yes; 5 No0 ÷ 50%: valid evidence that none of the five classified items is free.
3 Yes; 2 No3 ÷ 560%: three of five classified items are free.
0 Yes; 0 No; all Not applicable0 ÷ 0Undefined: the responses cannot establish a free-product percentage.

Coding the third example as zero would turn inapplicable information into confirmed nonfree provision.

5. Why the département average is undefined

We average valid facility proportions among the eligible FP providers. In Daoukro there are 19 eligible facilities, zero valid free-product proportions, and 19 undefined proportions. There are therefore no valid values to average.

We do not calculate 0 ÷ 19, because that would incorrectly replace the 19 undefined proportions with zeros. Pooling the product-response counts would not recover the information either: the pooled denominator is still zero.

6. What this means for the supply profile

Daily provision is also undefined in Daoukro. The frequency questions yield no recorded offered modern-method categories for the eligible facilities, so the daily/offered denominator is zero. The current assignment audit identifies both missing components: free-product provision and daily provision.

Other supply measures can still have values. However, the main index requires all six components, so Daoukro remains supply-unassessed. Missing values are not recoded zero, and no partial five- or four-component index is substituted.

Daoukro’s 153 SURGO women, including 43 women in need of FP, remain in the joined data and overview. They cannot enter the common regression sample requiring a complete supply score. The overall incomplete-profile counts in the preceding table are unchanged.

Interpretation for the research team

The aggregation follows the supplied UoM definitions and valid-response rule. The current eligibility filter identifies general FP-service providers; it does not require on-site method provision. Distinguish those populations when interpreting the supply index or considering a future eligibility revision. The present check explains the data; it does not change the selected eligibility rule, source responses, scores or fitted models.
