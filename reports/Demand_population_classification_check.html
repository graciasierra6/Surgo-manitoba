Does the demand population change geographic classification?

The current main classifications already use women in need of family planning. This separate check compares them with a hypothetical classification based on all eligible women. No main report, dataset or classification is overwritten.

Controlled comparison

  1. Keep the same six demand-domain definitions, valid-answer rules, geographic reference areas and supply measures/flags.
  2. Calculate weighted concern percentages within each area, first for all consenting women with positive final weights, then for the current need group.
  3. For each population separately, standardize each component across the same geographic areas using its mean and sample SD, then average six z-scores.
  4. Use demand score > 0 for the demand flag. Combine it with the unchanged supply-readiness flag to obtain the four categories.

This changes the demand population and its reference distribution together. It is not a comparison of regression samples. Need-group definitions remain original SURGO FPN2 for Lagos, Senegal and Côte d’Ivoire, and the authorized derived need flag for Sindh. Weighted estimates use positive final weights and consent where required. Areas with unassessed supply remain unassessed; five such Côte d’Ivoire areas are included in its total of 82.
