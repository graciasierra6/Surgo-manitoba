Each woman’s demand score averages her six responses standardized with final-weighted means and population SDs among valid women in need. Supply readiness averages her six district-wide favorable measures standardized against the 30-district planning references, after stockout burden becomes continuity = 1 − burden. Model supply risk is negative readiness; higher risk means weaker provision. Each woman retains personal demand variation, while women in one district share a supply score. All six responses are required; scores enter continuously in their original units.

Joint model. Demand OR 0.30 (0.22–0.42); supply OR 0.92 (0.77–1.09) per one score point. Interpret the intervals alongside the estimates. These associations do not allocate a percentage of nonuse to each side or establish a causal binding constraint.

Planning implication: Personal demand concerns are associated with modern use after adjustment. The selected supply index has an interval spanning no association; this does not show that improving supply has no value or determine an investment split. Limited training coverage is an additional reason for caution.

Curves average predictions over the same women and observed covariates. The other score stays observed. These are adjusted cross-sectional comparisons, not expected effects of an intervention.

Individual concerns: Personally opposes family planning — adjusted OR 0.10 (0.05–0.20); Low confidence choosing a method or managing side effects — adjusted OR 0.53 (0.42–0.67); Expects social disapproval of family planning — adjusted OR 0.64 (0.48–0.83). These are associations, not estimated intervention effects.

A result needing care: Needs permission to seek healthcare has a positive adjusted association (OR 1.28 (1.03–1.58)). This does not mean a barrier improves use. General healthcare permission, unmeasured circumstances and selection may differ from FP-specific access; these data are cross-sectional.

For each concern, predict every woman with that response present and absent, retaining other variables. Average using Normalizedweights and subtract; intervals account for covariance. The MC5 indicator measures confidence, despite the retained knowledge label.

What each supply measure represents

MeasureConstruction and interpretation
Facilities offering short- and long-acting options togetherMean of original one_larc_3_sa_offer: share meeting ≥1 long-acting plus ≥3 short-acting offering categories.
Products provided free (facility average)Mean of original p_free_prod: average facility fraction of recorded products provided free. Not the percentage of facilities providing a free method.
Trained-provider share (facility average)Mean of original p_train: average facility fraction of recorded personnel classified as trained under UoM topic/eligibility rules.
Offered modern methods provided daily (facility average)Within each FP facility: count of modern-method classes offered Daily / count offered Daily, Weekly, Fortnightly or Monthly; average valid facility ratios. Seven classes; IUD interval/post-abortion/postpartum contexts and injectable variants count once using the most frequent recorded offered variant. The entirely blank hormonal-IUD and Noristerat columns do not count as No responses.
Facilities with at least one LARC category in stockMean of original one_larc_stockin: share meeting the ≥1 LARC stock criterion. This is a current stock/readiness indicator, distinct from service frequency or stockout history.
Product groups with a coded stock interruption (facility average)Mean of original p_any_stockout: average facility share of coded product groups with a current/recent interruption under the UoM rule. Higher burden is worse. Not the share of facilities with any stockout.

The original cleaned stockout measure retains UoM product-group and missing-answer rules; numeric completeness does not establish that every underlying stock question was answered. No upstream stockout recoding is introduced. Q506 Yes is required before averaging. Each included facility contributes equally to its measure; no facility weights are used. No undefined facility value is zero-filled in this update.

Need definition retained

The authorized derived flag excludes the existing pregnancy, fertility, sexual-activity, postpartum and permanent-infertility categories. Its SA1 policy also excludes codes 95/97, although these special responses do not establish sexual inactivity. That approved rule is preserved here; no sterilization-based need_n additions are made. This is an instrument-based reconstruction, not a populated original SURGO FPN2 flag or the DHS unmet-need algorithm.
